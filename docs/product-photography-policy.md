# Product photography release policy

Standing owner instruction, 2026-11-26: every product thumbnail, product image, variant gallery, guide product image and product marketing image must be a real supplier photograph of the exact item sold, with only the actual orderable variant(s) represented. No generated art, stock substitutes, lookalike renders or generic category images. Missing evidence means no image; flag to the owner. Never infer supplier identity from Stripe URLs, product names or generic search results.

For each photo record the exact supplier listing URL and listing ID, selected SKU/variant ID, variant attributes, original photo URL, permission to use the photo, and date verified. Use approved supplier photos without altering product appearance. Verify displayed product, selected variant and fulfillment SKU match. If variants are not offered at checkout, show only the single fulfilled variant. For ambiguous or missing variant evidence, keep imagery absent and flag. Preserve checkout destinations and pricing unless separately authorized.

2026-11-26 audit: all twelve live product cards and buying-guide images used conceptual SVG artwork; no exact supplier listing or variant evidence was located in available source or prior handoffs. All product imagery removed pending provenance. See supplier-image-audit.json. No alternate image supplied.
